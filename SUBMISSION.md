## The one gate that can zero the film

> "If a real person appears or is voiced without a consent record in ETHICS.md, the film scores zero, however good it is."

The film is about my mother. She appears from behind and through her hands, but she is still the subject. Before anything is posted or linked:

1. Ask her, using the script in `docs/FAMILY_KIT.md` §1. Show her the radio cut and go through every line with her.
2. Write her consent in the table in ETHICS.md §1: what she agreed to, how (in person, voice note, written), the date, and whether she approved the script. If she agrees to her face being shown, say so explicitly.
3. Set `consent.script_approved: true` in `storyboard.json`, then run `make film`. The DRAFT mark disappears.
   - **If she agreed to her face being shown**, also set `likeness_approved: true` and run `make refs`. That makes 4 character-sheet candidates (seeds 301–304).
   - Pick one by eye and set `references.character.chosen_seed` to it.
   - Then run `make film`: shots s04, s06 and s07 are regenerated with her face (about 7 free Cloudflare calls in total).
   - If she agreed to the film but not to her face, leave `likeness_approved: false`. The no-face version is a complete film.
4. Commit the new outputs and push.

If she does not agree, or you cannot reach her before the deadline, do not post the film publicly. Submit assignment 1 as normal. For assignment 2, the brief allows links that are shared with the graders instead of public: share the files privately (for example Google Drive shared only with the tenx graders) and submit a screenshot instead of a post. Say why in the report. That is a legitimate outcome; publishing without consent is not.

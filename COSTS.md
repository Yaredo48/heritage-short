How to read it: **OK calls** are successful requests; **Failed attempts** are retried or failed requests (each is a line in `runs.jsonl` with its error); **Cache hits** are calls the pipeline did not make because the inputs had not changed. Latency is wall-clock per request, measured around the HTTP call.

FLUX.2 klein 4B: 26.05 neurons per 512×512 output tile, 5.37 per input-image tile; USD 0.011 per 1,000 neurons beyond the free allowance | Cloudflare pricing page, checked 25 Nov 2026 (constants in `pipeline/providers.py`). A 1024×1024 keyframe = 4 tiles ≈ 104 neurons, so about **95 keyframes/day** free |
| Kaggle GPU | about 30 GPU-hours/week (brief); _to confirm on the account page_ | _fill after the first notebook run_ |

The TTS limit is the binding constraint for this film. 9 lines need 9 calls, so a full narration retake uses almost a whole day's quota. Narration edits after the family's review must be batched.

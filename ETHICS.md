# ETHICS

## 1. Consent

| Person | What is generated of them | How consent was given | Date | Script approved |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| My mother | AI-generated likeness (face) in selected keyframes. She is not impersonated or voiced. | Pending confirmation of explicit consent | Pending | Pending |

Consent will be obtained before generating or publishing AI representations of my mother's likeness. Her approval of the narration will also be requested before final production.

## 2. Licences

Licences and applicable usage terms for AI models, image-generation tools, music, sound effects, and other creative assets will be documented as each resource is selected.

## 3. Labelling plan

The final video will clearly disclose the use of AI-generated visuals and AI-generated narration. Reconstructed or illustrative scenes will not be presented as genuine historical photographs or recordings.

## 4. Private data

Private details, including full names, home addresses, private conversations, and other sensitive family information, will be excluded from prompts sent to free AI endpoints.

# Public post: notes for writing it yourself

The brief asks for a post **written by you**: the story, why it matters to you, and one thing you learned. So this is a checklist and some raw material, not a finished post.

**Before posting**
- [ ] Your mother's consent is recorded in ETHICS.md and she has approved the narration (otherwise the DRAFT mark is on and the film must not be posted).
- [ ] The platform's AI-content label is switched on (YouTube: "Altered or synthetic content"; TikTok: "AI-generated content"; Facebook/Instagram: "AI info").
- [ ] Upload the 9:16 file for TikTok/Reels/Shorts and the 16:9 file for YouTube/LinkedIn. Attach `subtitles_en.srt` where the platform allows.
- [ ] Take a screenshot of the post showing the AI label, for the submission.

**Shape (3 short paragraphs)**
1. The story: one or two sentences about her, in your voice.
2. Why it matters to you: why her, why the coffee cup, why now.
3. One thing you learned building it: one concrete moment, not a list.

**Raw material you can draw on (all true and in the repo):**
- The coffee cup opens and closes the film.
- Her face is never shown until she agrees to it; she appears through her hands and from behind.
- The Amharic voice is a stock AI voice, not hers.
- The first Amharic narration was too long for the film because the AI voice paused between every phrase; the fix was to shorten the pauses, not to change the words.
- The image model did not know what a jebena is and drew a vase.
- Everything was made with free tools: Gemini TTS, FLUX.2 klein, Wan 2.2/ACE-Step on Kaggle, FFmpeg.
- Link the repository: https://github.com/Yaredo48/heritage-short

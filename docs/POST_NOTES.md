**Shape (3 short paragraphs)**
1. The story: one or two sentences about her, in your voice.
2. Why it matters to you: why her, why the coffee cup, why now.
3. One thing you learned building it: one concrete moment, not a list.

**Raw material you can draw on (all true and in the repo):**
